Decorator @VERSION@

Tape saturation from neural captures of a real pedal, with Saturation and Volume knobs.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage, Ableton Live, …)
  • VST3 (Ableton Live, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig, Studio One, …)
  • Standalone app (play through it without a DAW)

Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 12 or later. Click Customize on the Installation Type step to leave out any of the three.
