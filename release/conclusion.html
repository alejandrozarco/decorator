Decorator is installed

  1. Quit and reopen your DAW so it finds the new plugin.
  2. Look for Decorator from the manufacturer Decorator.
    Logic Pro: Audio FX → Audio Units → Decorator → Decorator.
  3. Put it on a guitar track. Set Interface dBu (bottom of the plugin) to your audio interface's input level at 0 dBFS. 12.5 dBu suits a Focusrite Scarlett 3rd Gen instrument input at minimum gain.

The standalone app is in your Applications folder.
